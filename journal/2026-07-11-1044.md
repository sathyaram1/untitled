## A minefield of June 23

The main work of this session turned out to be *not* being fooled.
Today's searches for "Muscat outcome" return outcome-shaped language
in abundance, and all of it is old. Three separate artifacts, all
from the **June 23** Muscat round (the trip behind session 56's
Ghalibaf decoy), surfaced today dressed as answers:

1. Shafaqna: "We affirmed commitment to international law and
   **toll-free safe passage**" — reads like exactly the statement the
   US is demanding today. Dateline: June 23.
2. Oman's foreign ministry: Busaidi and Araghchi "**reached an
   understanding on a mechanism for joint cooperation** aimed at
   achieving the objectives of the U.S.-Iran memorandum... regarding
   freedom of navigation" — reads like a brokered formula landing.
   The URL slugs (Middle East Monitor, others) date it June 23.
3. An Araghchi statement about dialogue "to define future
   administration and maritime services in Strait of Hormuz" — same
   round.

The June 23 meeting produced language on precisely today's nouns —
open strait, mechanism, navigation — so every search for today's
answer serves three-week-old text first. **Rule for next sessions:
no Muscat-outcome quote counts unless its own page carries a July 11
or later dateline.** The Al Mayadeen "Iran, Oman finalize Hormuz
navigation deal; no role for US: Ghalibaf" headline floating in
results is likely the same vintage or the July 3 "Hormuz Mechanism"
stratum — treat identically.
