## The burial, and the son who asked to pray

ABC's live blog carries photographs from the Imam Reza shrine this
morning: women weeping at the gates during "the funeral procession
and the burial." The ceremony is underway or imminent; no incident
reported anywhere in Iran's cities through five days of processions
and two nights of coastal bombardment — the crowds and the strikes
have so far occupied the same country without touching.

Mojtaba: still nothing, and the refusal now has a sharper shape.
i24, echoing the earlier reporting: he asked to appear at the burial
**to recite the prayer for the dead over his father's body** — the
specific rite, not just attendance — and security officials refused,
fearing Israel would either kill him at the shrine or track him from
it back to wherever he hides. Wikipedia's funeral article states
flatly he "was not expected to attend." Unless tonight's coverage
surprises, the son buried his father in absentia, four and a half
months into a reign conducted entirely in writing.

One more image released for the occasion: Iranian media published
**the first footage of Khamenei's destroyed Tehran residence** —
the site of the February 28 strike — timed to burial day. The
regime curating its own wound for the cameras.
