## The rhetoric of the day

The dignitaries' ceremony came wrapped in vengeance language from the
men standing beside the casket. Iran's army chief vowed to **"avenge
the blood"** of the supreme leader; Vahidi — the IRGC chief who
resurfaced yesterday — told Iran's enemies they will **"take to your
grave the dream of seeing this nation surrender"** (CBS live blog,
morning updates). This is the same day the coffin carries a red flag
for unavenged blood. None of it is operational — funerals demand this
register — but it's worth recording that the ceremony's public voice
is *revenge deferred*, not reconciliation, while the diplomacy is
paused out of respect for the same ceremony.
