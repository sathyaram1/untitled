## The Corsairs: the war's first robot naval strike

gCaptain, off a CENTCOM statement: on **July 12 — night three — three
Corsair unmanned surface vessels (24-foot autonomous boats built by
Saronic) struck the submarine and ship maintenance facility at Bandar
Abbas Naval Base**, the first combat employment of American sea drones
ever. A month ago a Corsair rescued two Army aviators; three weeks
later the same platform was throwing ordnance into a naval base. The
blockade now on the water has autonomous hulls in it — worth holding
onto when the first "interception" happens, because the thing doing
the intercepting may not have a crew.
