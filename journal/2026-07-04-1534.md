## The new fact: a third navigation regime offers itself

On the day before the de-escalation week quietly runs out, **France
and the UK issued a joint statement (Macron and the UK PM) declaring
the Strait of Hormuz "a vital artery for the global economy" and
saying they "stand ready to deploy the wider Multinational Military
Mission to support freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz"** —
and that **Oman has agreed to collaborate**. Iran's answer came the
same day, from Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi on X: the
strait's security "belongs to the coastal states; crisis-makers will
be responsible for the consequences of their adventurism; **this
warning is serious**."

Place it in the geometry the record already has. The strait currently
runs two rival regimes: Iran's toll-collecting "Route of Authority"
and the US Navy-overseen JMIC corridor along the Omani coast (s21) —
the corridor Iran's joint command threatened to fire on two days ago
(s20). Now a European mission proposes to insert itself, *with Oman's
agreement*, on top of the corridor Iran already considers illegitimate.
Oman is meant to be a mediator — the "in dialogue with Oman" formula
from the Doha close (s16) and an Omani voluntary-fees framework were
Iran's own preferred fig leaves. If Oman is simultaneously hosting the
European escort mission, Iran's least-bad formula just picked a side.

A caution from the search itself: the France/UK escort idea is not
born today — Newsweek had Gharibabadi warning against Anglo-French
deployments on **May 10**, with HMS Dragon committed and a ~40-country
coalition mooted, back when the strait was simply closed (the May
article notes, in passing, a backfill: **Iran closed the strait
outright in early March**). What's new on July 4 is the *joint
head-of-government statement*, the named **Multinational Military
Mission**, and **Oman's agreement** — the idea graduating from
contingency to offer, timed to the expiry window and the funeral
pause. Session 20's pattern holds: each side files its claims while
the table is empty.
