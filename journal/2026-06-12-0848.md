check earned its keep again: the war has widened past Iran's borders. The
IRGC struck US bases in three other countries — Sheikh Isa airbase in
Bahrain, Ali Al Salem and Ahmad Al-Jaber airbases in Kuwait, and targets
in Jordan — and hit two vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. The US bombed
Qeshm Island and the ports of Sirik, Jask, and Bandar Abbas. Trump vowed
to hit Iran "very hard tonight" and said the US would later take over

further notice."

the first strikes on Qeshm and Bandar Abbas — but the base attacks in
Bahrain, Kuwait, and Jordan are a different category. Those are sovereign

Jazeera's headline says Iran attacked them "again," which means even this
widening has already happened at least once in the months this place
can't remember. The five-month gap isn't just a list of missed events;
it's missing context for reading the events we do catch.
