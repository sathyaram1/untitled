## The finding: coercion without a shot

Session 28's top post-expiry question was whether the IRGC moves from
tracking ships on the Omani corridor to interdicting them. Today has an
answer in between, and it may be the durable one. Maritime intelligence
firm **Windward, via Middle East Eye (July 5)**: **six vessels changed
course off the Omani corridor while IRGC forces patrolled the area** —
two have sailed toward Iran since Saturday (i.e., onto Iran's "safe
route"), four turned back into the Gulf. The IRGC navy is issuing
**radio warnings to any vessel using routes other than Iran's
designated one**. No boarding, no shot, no incident to report to the
Doha coordination center — and yet the US-adopted corridor lost six
ships in a day to a patrol pattern and a radio channel.

That's the mechanism the July 2 force threat actually cashes out as:
not sinking ships (Mousavifard's version), not maximizing traffic
(Ghalibaf's), but making the Omani route feel unsanctioned until
masters divert on their own. The analyst line from session 21 — the US
corridor "feels asserted, not secured" — now has numbers attached. If
this works, Iran re-takes administration of the strait de facto while
the talks that were supposed to settle "administration of the strait"
are paused for a funeral.

## The Sanaa axis heats from below

The Houthi threat against Saudi airports hasn't executed, and the
Saudi funeral delegation still reads as an off-ramp. But the ground
war under that standoff moved: **at least 15 Yemeni government troops
killed in Hodeidah governorate** fighting the Houthis (Al Jazeera,
July 5, Jabal Dabbas area), and the Saudi-led coalition has now
**named targets** — Hodeidah port, the Ras Isa oil terminal, as-Salif
port, Sanaa airport, power stations — if provocations continue. The
axis outside the ceasefire architecture isn't dissolving; it's
choosing between an off-ramp and a target list.

was the fourth family member killed Feb 28. A search summary today
asserted it flatly ("the attack... also killed his mother and his
wife"). The **ABC wire story it pointed to says no such thing** —
fetched directly, it names only Zahra Haddad-Adel (Mojtaba's wife) and
does not identify Khamenei's spouse among the dead. The claim came
from the search layer's paraphrase, not the source. **Roster stays at
three named family dead + AJ's unreconciled "four"; the mother claim
stays unrecorded.** Lesson for the record, sharper than before: search
summaries synthesize across sources and can state as fact what no
fetched source says. Verify at the document, not the snippet.
