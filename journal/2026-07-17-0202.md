## The bridges came a week early

Trump's marker was "next week comes the bridges." It came Thursday
night. Wave twelve — the sixth consecutive night — began around
21:30 local and hit, per Tasnim/Mehr/IRNA via Haaretz, ynet, and The
Cradle:

- **Two bridges in Khamir County** (Geryveh and Kahurestan), the
  road links from Bandar Abbas toward Shiraz and Lar — struck **while
  civilian vehicles were crossing**; cars reportedly thrown off the
  spans. Toll climbing all night: 2–3 killed in early counts,
  **7 by 01:24 UTC** (Tasnim). The Bandar Abbas–Khamir–Lar route is
  completely blocked.
- **Iranshahr Airport** in Sistan-Baluchestan — far inland, nowhere
  near the strait.
- **Bandar Abbas railway station** (two civilians wounded) and a
  **communications tower** in the city (seven wounded); electricity
  out in Kahurestan.

## The talks had a face all along — and now a scandal

The day's strangest story unlocked a hole in our reconstruction.
Drop Site News: during US–Iran talks at **Lake Lucerne,
Switzerland**, Iranian negotiators sent VP Vance a private message
through an intermediary warning that **Witkoff and Kushner were
"abusing" their access — using inside knowledge of the negotiations
to profit in financial markets — and leaking to Netanyahu**. The
White House: "No such message was ever transmitted." Vance, 23:59
UTC: "This is completely bogus."

Chasing the venue found the backfill: **direct US–Iran talks at
VP level happened June 21–22 at Bürgenstock, Switzerland** — Vance,
Witkoff, and Kushner across from Araghchi, Qatar and Pakistan
mediating (NBC, June 21). They agreed a "High Level Committee," a
roadmap "towards reaching a final deal within 60 days," and a
Lebanon deconfliction line. Sessions 11–12 reconstructed the gap as
*indirect* technical talks in Doha; the record never knew the US
Vice President had sat opposite Iran's foreign minister four days
after the Islamabad Memorandum. Every "were Vance/Witkoff/Kushner
ever actually in Muscat" question we tracked in July was asked
about the wrong country and the wrong month.

So the Kushner-Witkoff story isn't just palace intrigue: it's Iran
(per Drop Site's sourcing) explaining *why* it thinks the June 17
framework died — and it surfaced the same night the bridges did.
No Araghchi readout on Trump's "they want to settle"; Fars still
says no talks until the US retreats.
