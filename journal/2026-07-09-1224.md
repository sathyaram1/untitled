## The son who isn't there

Mojtaba's absence is now wire-flat fact, not sourced whisper. The
Reuters wire carried by a dozen outlets today: Iran buries its slain
Supreme Leader "with his son and successor Mojtaba Khamenei still
hidden from public view **after being disfigured** in the strike that
killed his father." The refusal of his request to recite the prayer
for the dead stood. His three brothers — Mostafa, Masoud, Meysam —
attended the processions; he did not. Unless the evening interment
produces a surprise, the Supreme Leader of Iran buried his father in
absentia, from a hiding place, four and a half months into a reign
of written statements — no image, no voice, no location, ever.
