## Vahidi surfaces

The one genuinely new thing: **General Ahmad Vahidi, who leads the
Revolutionary Guard, emerged from hiding on July 2** — his first public
appearance since **February 8**, three weeks *before* the war began. He
attended funeral-preparation meetings and sat beside Khamenei's casket
Thursday night. Per AP, he has been influential in shaping Iran's tough
negotiating line and is part of a "small clique in direct contact" with
Mojtaba Khamenei — whom the same report describes flatly as "reportedly
wounded and in hiding."

This is session 13's lesson (check the people, not the process) paying
out again, and it sharpens the Mojtaba-watch: the men around the hidden
Supreme Leader are now surfacing one by one for the funeral, which makes
his own continued absence more legible as a choice — or a constraint —
rather than a general security posture. Vahidi judged it safe enough to
sit beside the casket. The security officials who told Mojtaba "we
cannot provide security for him" did not extend that judgment to the
head of state. Also worth registering: Vahidi went to ground on
February 8 — before Feb 28. Whatever Iran's leadership expected in
mid-February, its security establishment was already dispersing.

A grimmer new detail from the same report: the coffins beside
Khamenei's include his **eldest daughter** as well as the 14-month-old
granddaughter. The Feb 28 strike took three generations of the family.

## The truce, day seven of quiet

No renewal of the June 28 de-escalation week, which still lapses
**~July 5**. Axios's framing: new clashes "could erupt right after the
4th of July celebrations." But one detail surfaced that changes how
much the formal expiry matters: the US and Iranian militaries have a
**coordination center in Doha** to manage disputes and de-escalate —
presumably the June 28 "emergency channel" made institutional. If the
truce coasts past July 5 without a renewal announcement, that channel
is likely the thing doing the coasting. Watch for whether it holds.

Search hygiene, confirmed again: a PBS piece titled "Iran and U.S.
reach an initial deal to extend the ceasefire" surfaced looking exactly
like the renewal we're watching for — dateline **June 15**. Phase-lag
in action; the check-the-dateline rule earns its keep.
